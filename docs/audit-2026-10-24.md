# Site audit, 2026-10-24

Taken at the end of the v1.2.1 day, against the live site (redlamp.github.io/crosspost), the static build in `out/`, and the code. Most of it was fixed the next morning (see "Fixed"); what's left is below it.

## Fixed on 2026-10-24 (released as v1.2.2)

1. **"This page couldn't load" after a deploy.** GitHub Pages keeps the page's HTML for 10 minutes (`max-age=600`); a deploy in that window replaces the hashed chunks the kept page names, so a visitor with a stale page hit 404s and Next's error screen. The service worker now asks the host for page loads afresh (`cache: "no-cache"`; the host answers 304 when nothing changed) and leaves hashed assets to the browser's cache. Two tests in `src/lib/sw.test.ts`.
2. **Deliver's tab panel named by a tab that isn't there.** The panel gets the `tabpanel` role and its label only when there's more than one way (there's one, with the agent parked).
3. **Light theme contrast.** `--brand-blue-text` (oklch 0.5 0.16 246) for the blue as words (the Start here wave, a Copied button); the dark theme keeps the logo's blue. `--muted-foreground` nudged from 0.556 to 0.53 in the light theme (4.43:1 to about 4.9:1 at 14px).
4. **Tap targets.** The cards' count hints have a 28px hit area around the 16px icon, the Number posts switch a 24px-tall one, and the Try an example and version links are 24px tall.
5. **The tour shipped to everyone.** The dialog loads apart from the page (`src/components/intro-loader.tsx`): ahead of time while the welcome card shows, on its hover or focus, or on the click (the step is queued until it's in; a folded card waits for a reach). The steps and trail the card needs moved to `src/lib/tour-steps.ts`. Initial JS went from 268KB to 248KB gzipped; the tour's own chunk is 18KB gzipped.
6. **The spec** caught up on all of the above and on the v1.2.1 fold work.

Also from the phone feedback the same morning: every step keeps the same shape (a tabs row kept on every step, Decide's text under its drawing, two lines' room for the title on phones, one dialog height on phones), the camera carries its place from one drawing to the next instead of cutting, Decide's camera follows the pointer, and Deliver's phone drawing rests before the tour moves on with a timing guess of its own (the marker reaches 95% of the way, was 83%).

## Checked and fine

- Clean load: no console errors; `lang="en"`, title, description, `og:image`, `theme-color`, and the manifest all present; the four manifest icons answer 200; the share target path is right; unknown paths get Crosspost's own 404 page.
- No duplicate ids; every button and link has a name (the Number posts switch is named by its label); every image has alt; headings go H1 then H2.
- No horizontal overflow at 375px; the text box stays 16px there, so iOS won't zoom it.
- Dark theme contrast is 5:1 or better everywhere measured.
- The service worker is registered with `updateViaCache: "none"`, so a changed `sw.js` is picked up on the next visit; Clear Data resets other open tabs too.
- CSS is 92KB raw; the fonts were two subsetted woff2 files (ten subset faces since the fonts went local, the two latin ones preloaded).
